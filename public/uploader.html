Ball Duel Uploader

A desktop publishing tool for the official Ball Duel YouTube channel.

Privacy PolicyPlay Ball DuelContact

For the channel operator. This tool is separate from the browser game. Players can enjoy Ball Duel without connecting a Google account.

What the tool does

  • Uploads selected local Ball Duel videos and their captions to the authorized YouTube channel.
  • Keeps a local record of successful uploads to help avoid uploading the same clip twice.
  • Reads the channel identity and the uploaded videos’ status, views, likes, and comment counts.

How Google authorization works

The operator runs the tool on their own computer and signs in through Google’s authorization page. The tool requests YouTube upload and read-only permissions. It never requests the Google password directly. Authorization does not upload a video by itself; uploading requires a separate command.

Saved credentials stay on the operator’s device outside the source repository. See our Google and YouTube data practices for the precise permissions, storage, retention, sharing, and deletion options.

Access and support

This is an operator-run tool for the official Ball Duel channel, not a public sign-up service. For questions about the tool or its data practices, email ballduel.game@gmail.com.

You can revoke its access in Google Account connections. The YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy also apply to the Google and YouTube services you use.
